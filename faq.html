NepaliEcoChat.bot

Dear User,

What is NepaliEcoChat.bot?

NepaliEcoChat.bot is an AI-powered assistant that helps individuals and communities across Nepal access real-time environmental data, local waste management guidance, and sustainable living recommendations in both Nepali and English.

How do I start chatting with the bot?

Tap the "Chat Now" button at the top of the page to start a conversation on WhatsApp. You can ask questions in Nepali or English and get instant responses.

Is my data safe with NepaliEcoChat.bot?

We take your privacy seriously. Please review our Privacy Policy for full details on how your information is collected, used, and protected.

Which districts are currently supported?

We are actively expanding coverage across Nepal. Check back regularly as new districts and local guides are added to the platform.

Where can I learn more or contribute?

Visit our community page on GitHub to explore the project, report issues, or contribute.

Who do I contact for support?

Reach out to us anytime at contact@nepaliecochat.bot and our team will get back to you.
